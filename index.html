George Larson · Director of Technology

Twenty-five years building the systems that get expensive when they break.

Logistics to developer at Sony. PLCs on the production line. A medical-billing platform grown from fifty people to national volume. Technology director through the good years and the lean ones.

The thread was never the title. Titles came and went. The work held steady: take the messy, costly, breaks-at-3am stuff, automate the ripe parts, dig into the problems underneath.

Much of that doesn't make impressive screenshots. The provider that stalls mid-call without throwing an error. The task runner wedged on a channel that never times out. The reconciliation that quietly disagrees with itself. That's where the serious work lives.

AI: used heavily for what it does well, and stated plainly. The proof it's used well is you can't find the seams.

  • The n8n stuck-channel hunt A silent failure that passed every health check. The fix drops detection from hours to about six seconds. Submitted upstream as PR #29516.
