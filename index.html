George Larson · Director of Technology

Twenty-five years building the systems that get expensive when they break.

Logistics to developer at Sony. PLCs on the production line. A medical-billing platform grown from fifty people to national volume. Technology director through the good years and the lean ones. Production AI shipped inside the business from 2023: a design assistant customers used, a knowledge platform, a bridge across two vendor pipelines.

The failures that hurt most are the quiet ones. Catching them early is the work.
